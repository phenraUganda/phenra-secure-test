ARRC Rare Disease Triage (PHENRA) is a research prototype for frontline recognition of unexplained phenotypes. It does not diagnose rare disease and does not replace disease-specific clinical assessment. Known infectious or chronic disease must be evaluated for explanatory sufficiency before rare-disease escalation.
